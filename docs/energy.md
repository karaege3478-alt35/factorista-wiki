# Energy (FE)

## Generation

- **Generator:** solid fuel or liquid fuel (diesel, gasoline, lubricant). 10,000 FE buffer.
- **Solar panel:** 10 FE/s. Industrial: 80 FE/s. Needs open sky.
- **Wind turbine:** 0-40 FE/s by altitude, needs sky.
- **Water turbine:** 8 FE/s per touching water (max 48).
- **Nuclear reactor:** 24,000 FE per rod. Needs heat management; runs forever with lubricant cooling.

## Transport

Wires are tiered (Paper 5, Copper 20, Gold 50, Diamond 100, HV 500 FE/s) and **tiers do not connect to each other**. The slowest wire on the path limits the machine.
